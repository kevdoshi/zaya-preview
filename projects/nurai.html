Nurai

A pure private island paradise.

Nurai, derived from the Arabic word ‘nur’, meaning ‘light,’ Zaya Nurai Island was conceived as an ultra-luxury private island resort, redefining hospitality and exclusivity in the UAE. Launched in 2008 for residential sales, Nurai Island set a new standard for real estate in Abu Dhabi, with property sales reaching 3,000 AED per square foot, double the market average at the time.

Our story

Rooted in the earth yet connected to the stars.

2005

Al Barari

Al Barari, derived from the Arabic word for ‘wilderness’, was established in 2005 as the region’s first integrated luxury and eco-conscious development.

2008

Established in 2008, ZAYA is more than a leading boutique real estate firm.

Launched in 2008 for residential sales, Nurai Island set a new standard for real estate in Abu Dhabi.

2014

Nurai Island

Officially opening as a resort in 2014, Nurai Island quickly became a sanctuary of serenity and elegance, just a 15-minute boat ride from Abu Dhabi.

2017

Al Fanar School

Founded in 2017, Al Fanar School was created to lead an educational shift in the UAE.

2022

Al Barari

Demonstrating its market appeal, Al Barari achieved sales totaling 3.3 billion AED between 2020 and 2022.

Today

Zuhha Island

Derived from the Arabic word ‘Zuhha’, meaning “morning light,” ZUHHA Island embodies inspiration and elegance, setting a new standard for private island living in the Emirates.

$6 billion

ZAYA holds a peerless track record of delivering over $6 billion worth of extraordinary real estate and transformative experiences.

At ZAYA, we don’t sell ideas. We build them.

Passion. Innovation & Excellence

In Dubai, many companies have bosses in boardrooms. We have leaders on the ground.

Nadia Zaal

Founder, Right Brain & CEO

Nadia Zaal is a pioneering Emirati entrepreneur and the founder and CEO of Zaya Group, a boutique developer specializing in high-end real estate and hospitality.

As the former CEO of Al Barari Group, Nadia spearheaded the creation of Al Barari, a lush, low-density community renowned for its focus on sustainability and eco-conservation.

Adib Moukheiber

COO & The Hand

Adib Moukheiber, COO of Zaya, brings over 25 years of expertise in luxury hospitality and hotel management to his leadership role.

Formerly the General Manager of the award-winning Zaya Nurai Island, Adib played a crucial role in establishing the resort as Abu Dhabi’s most celebrated private island destination.
